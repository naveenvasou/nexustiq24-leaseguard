COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT
Premises: Suite 400, Highland Tower, 100 Financial Way
Landlord: Highland Commercial Realty LLC
Tenant: Apex Property Management Solutions Inc.
Commencement Date: October 1, 2026

This Commercial Lease Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into between Highland Commercial Realty LLC ("Landlord") and Apex Property Management Solutions Inc. ("Tenant").

Clause 1: Base Rent and Annual Escalation
The initial base rent shall be $8,500.00 per calendar month, payable on or before the first day of each month. The base rent shall remain fixed and unchanged for the initial twelve (12) months of the Lease Term. Beginning on the thirteenth (13th) month and on each subsequent anniversary of the Commencement Date, base rent shall increase by three and one-half percent (3.5%) per annum over the preceding year's base rent. No mid-term escalations or arbitrary market-rate adjustments shall be applied during the term.

Clause 2: Security Deposit and Escrow
Upon execution of this Agreement, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord an amount of $17,000.00, representing exactly two (2) months of base rent, as a security deposit. Landlord covenants and agrees to hold said funds in a segregated, interest-bearing escrow account at a federally insured commercial bank, with all interest earned credited to Tenant. Within twenty-five (25) calendar days following the expiration of the Lease Term and delivery of possession, Landlord shall fully refund the security deposit, together with accrued interest, less only documented costs of repair for physical damages caused by Tenant beyond reasonable wear and tear.

Clause 3: Notice Period for Renewal and Non-Renewal
Either party may terminate or elect not to renew this Agreement upon the expiration of the Lease Term by delivering written notice to the other party at least sixty (60) calendar days prior to the expiration date. In the event Landlord proposes any modification of terms or rental rates for an extension term, Landlord shall submit written proposals at least sixty (60) days in advance. Both parties are subject to identical and symmetrical notice requirements.

Clause 4: Maintenance and Capital Repairs
Landlord shall, at its sole cost and expense throughout the Lease Term, keep, maintain, repair, and replace the foundation, exterior walls, structural columns, load-bearing floors, roof, common facilities, and central utility systems (including HVAC units, plumbing mains, electrical switchboards, and life safety equipment). Tenant shall only be responsible for routine housekeeping and non-structural interior maintenance within the demised premises where individual repairs do not exceed $200.00 per occurrence.

Clause 5: Subletting and Assignment Rights
Tenant shall have the unrestricted right to assign this Lease or sublet the demised premises or any portion thereof to any corporate parent, subsidiary, affiliate, or successor entity resulting from merger or reorganization without requiring Landlord's consent or payment of any transfer fee. Tenant may sublet or assign to qualified third parties with Landlord's prior written consent, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed beyond ten (10) business days. Failure of Landlord to respond within ten (10) business days shall constitute deemed consent.

Clause 6: Lock-in Period and Early Termination
The initial lock-in period shall be six (6) calendar months from the Commencement Date. Following the expiration of said six-month lock-in period, Tenant shall possess the right to terminate this Lease for business convenience at any time by delivering sixty (60) days' prior written notice to Landlord. No early termination fee, liquidated damages, or accelerated rent obligations shall be assessed against Tenant for exercising this exit right.

Clause 7: Default, Notice, and Opportunity to Cure
In the event of any alleged failure by Tenant to pay rent or any other monetary sum when due, Landlord shall provide formal written notice, and Tenant shall have thirty (30) calendar days from receipt of said notice to cure such default. For any non-monetary covenant default, Tenant shall have forty-five (45) calendar days following written notice to effectuate a cure. Landlord shall have no right of re-entry, lockout, or lease forfeiture prior to the expiration of the applicable cure period.

Clause 8: Tenant Alterations and Cosmetic Improvements
Tenant is authorized to perform non-structural interior alterations, decorative improvements, wall partitioning, data/voice cabling, and corporate signage costing up to $10,000.00 in the aggregate without Landlord's prior consent or payment of review fees. Any structural alterations shall require Landlord's prior written approval, not to be unreasonably withheld. Upon surrender of the premises, Tenant shall not be required to remove non-structural improvements or restore the space to bare shell condition.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this Agreement as of the date first above written.
