COMMERCIAL OFFICE LEASE AGREEMENT
Premises: Suite 250, Bayview Business Center, 75 Harbor Point Way
Landlord: Bayview Properties Corporation
Tenant: Apex Property Management Solutions Inc.
Commencement Date: December 1, 2026

This Commercial Office Lease Agreement is entered into between Bayview Properties Corporation ("Landlord") and Apex Property Management Solutions Inc. ("Tenant").

Clause 1: Base Rent and Annual Escalation
Tenant shall pay monthly base rent in the amount of $6,200.00. Base rent shall remain fixed for the first twelve (12) months. On each annual anniversary of the Commencement Date, base rent shall adjust upwards by an escalation factor of four percent (4.0%) per year.

Clause 2: Security Deposit
Tenant shall provide a security deposit equivalent to two (2) months' rent ($12,400.00) upon signing. The deposit shall be maintained in an escrow account, and Landlord shall return the full deposit within thirty (30) calendar days after lease termination, less reasonable repair deductions for damage exceeding normal wear and tear.

Clause 3: Notice Period
Either party wishing to conclude or not renew this tenancy upon expiration of the primary term must serve written notice upon the other party at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration date.

Clause 4: Maintenance and Operating Services
Landlord covenants to maintain the building structure, exterior envelope, roof, and central heating and air-conditioning machinery in good working order. Tenant shall be responsible for routine interior cleaning and minor maintenance inside the leased suite.

Clause 5: Term and Initial Lock-in
The initial lease term shall include a lock-in commitment of six (6) months. Following six months of occupancy, Tenant shall have the privilege of terminating the lease upon providing sixty (60) days' advance written notice without early termination penalties.

Clause 6: Default and Opportunity to Cure
Should Tenant default in the timely payment of rent, Landlord shall provide written notice and allow Tenant thirty (30) calendar days to cure said delinquency. For any non-monetary default, Tenant shall be granted forty-five (45) calendar days from receipt of written notice to cure before Landlord may take remedial legal action.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, Landlord and Tenant execute this Lease Agreement.
