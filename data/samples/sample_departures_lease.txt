COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE LEASE AGREEMENT
Premises: Unit 1200, Metro Plaza Tower, 500 Commerce Boulevard
Landlord: Metropolitan Plaza Properties LP
Tenant: Apex Property Management Solutions Inc.
Commencement Date: November 1, 2026

This Commercial Real Estate Lease Agreement is entered into by Metropolitan Plaza Properties LP ("Landlord") and Apex Property Management Solutions Inc. ("Tenant").

Clause 1: Base Rent and Rent Adjustments
Tenant agrees to pay initial base rent of $12,000.00 per month. The base rent shall increase by twelve percent (12.0%) per annum, compounding annually, with the first such escalation taking effect at the conclusion of month six (6) of the initial term. Furthermore, Landlord reserves the absolute and unilateral right at the end of each operating year to re-evaluate base rent to prevailing open-market asking rates as determined solely by Landlord's independent appraisal.

Clause 2: Security Deposit Requirements
Tenant shall tender upon signature a security deposit equal to five (5) months of gross rent ($60,000.00). Landlord shall hold this deposit in its general operating bank account, and funds may be commingled with other landlord operating revenues. No interest shall accrue or be payable to Tenant. Following surrender of the premises, Landlord shall have ninety (90) business days to inspect the premises and return any remaining balance after discretionary deduction of administrative fees and refurbishment allowances.

Clause 3: Notice Requirements for Termination or Non-Renewal
In order to terminate or prevent automatic renewal of this Lease at term end, Tenant must deliver written notice via certified mail at least one hundred twenty (120) calendar days prior to expiration. Conversely, Landlord may elect not to renew or may terminate this Lease for any operational reason by providing fifteen (15) calendar days' written notice to Tenant.

Clause 4: Maintenance, Repairs, and Capital Replacement
Tenant acknowledges that this is a strict absolute net lease. Tenant shall be solely responsible for all maintenance, routine repairs, and major capital replacements required on the property. Specifically, Tenant shall be responsible for replacing or overhauling central HVAC compressors, repairing roof membrane punctures, maintaining structural foundations, and paying a proportionate share of external structural facade refurbishments exceeding $50,000.00.

Clause 5: Absolute Prohibition of Assignment and Subletting
Tenant shall not assign, mortgage, pledge, encumber, license, or sublet the leased premises or any part thereof under any circumstances whatsoever. Any merger, consolidation, acquisition, sale of equity interests, or internal corporate restructuring involving Tenant shall be deemed an unauthorized assignment and shall constitute an incurable event of immediate default, entitling Landlord to terminate the Lease.

Clause 6: Non-Cancellable Lock-in Period
Tenant is bound by a strict, mandatory lock-in period of thirty-six (36) consecutive months from the Commencement Date. In the event Tenant vacates or attempts early termination prior to the completion of the 36-month lock-in period, all base rent and operating expenses for the entire remaining unexpired term shall immediately accelerate and become due as liquidated damages, and Tenant's security deposit shall be automatically forfeited.

Clause 7: Default and Landlord Remedies
If Tenant fails to make any rental payment within three (3) business days of its due date, or breaches any covenant herein, Landlord may, without further notice and without any grace or cure period, immediately terminate this Lease, revoke access badges, and re-enter and re-possess the premises by changing locks. Tenant hereby waives all statutory rights to formal cure notices and eviction hearings.

Clause 8: Alterations, Installations, and Bare-Shell Restoration
Tenant shall not install any partition, wall hanging, signage, electrical outlet, or low-voltage data cabling without Landlord's prior written consent. Tenant shall pay Landlord a non-refundable administrative engineering review fee of $1,500.00 for each alteration request. Upon lease termination or surrender, Tenant shall, at its sole cost and expense, demolish and remove all tenant improvements and restore the premises to bare concrete shell condition.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have signed this Agreement.
