# Apex Property Management Legal Desk: Standard Positions Reference Manual

This document establishes the official standard legal positions (SP-1 through SP-8) of the Property Management Legal Desk. Every incoming lease agreement must be systematically reviewed against these benchmarks before execution.

---

### [SP-1] Rent Escalation: Annual Rent Escalation Cap
- **Standard Position**: Base rent remains fixed for the initial 12 months. Any annual escalation thereafter shall not exceed 5.0% per annum or the Consumer Price Index (CPI-U), whichever is lower. Mid-term or discretionary escalations are prohibited.
- **Acceptable Range**: Fixed year 1; annual escalation <= 5.0% post-month 12.
- **Departure Indicators**: Escalation > 5.0%, compounding multi-tier indices, semi-annual increases, landlord unilateral rate resets.
- **Silence Risk**: Common-law open-ended rent adjustments or unilateral rent resets upon lease renewal.

---

### [SP-2] Security Deposit: Deposit Amount and Escrow Return
- **Standard Position**: Security deposit shall not exceed two (2) months' base rent. Must be placed in a segregated, interest-bearing escrow account with interest payable to tenant. Full return required within thirty (30) days of lease expiration less itemized documented damages.
- **Acceptable Range**: <= 2 months' base rent; FDIC escrow required; refund timeline <= 30 days.
- **Departure Indicators**: Deposit > 2 months, commingling with landlord operating funds, withholding interest, refund windows > 30 days.
- **Silence Risk**: Commingled landlord funds, indefinite retention of deposit, arbitrary deductions without receipts.

---

### [SP-3] Notice Period: Mutual Notice for Renewal and Termination
- **Standard Position**: Mutual written notice of at least sixty (60) days prior to expiration is required for termination, non-renewal, or lease modification. Symmetrical obligations apply to both parties.
- **Acceptable Range**: Mutual notice >= 60 days.
- **Departure Indicators**: Landlord notice < 60 days (e.g. 15 or 30 days), tenant notice requirement > 60 days, asymmetric notice rights.
- **Silence Risk**: Statutory month-to-month conversion disputes, abrupt eviction notices, or unwanted automatic renewals.

---

### [SP-4] Maintenance and Repairs: Structural and Capital Allocation
- **Standard Position**: Landlord maintains and repairs all structural components, foundation, roof, exterior walls, building utility infrastructure (HVAC, plumbing, electrical), and common areas. Tenant is responsible only for minor interior maintenance under $250 per occurrence.
- **Acceptable Range**: Landlord responsible for structural/roof/HVAC; tenant minor interior threshold <= $250.
- **Departure Indicators**: Tenant required to repair or replace HVAC/roof/foundation, full NNN capital pass-through without amortization.
- **Silence Risk**: High-risk exposure to sudden, catastrophic structural and capital repair bills.

---

### [SP-5] Subletting and Assignment: Transfer Rights with Reasonable Consent
- **Standard Position**: Tenant holds unqualified right to assign or sublet to affiliates, subsidiaries, or corporate successors without consent. Third-party subletting requires landlord consent, which must not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed beyond 14 days.
- **Acceptable Range**: Affiliate transfers permitted freely; third-party sublet consent not unreasonably withheld/delayed (<= 14 days).
- **Departure Indicators**: Absolute prohibition on sublease, arbitrary consent discretion, triggering lease default upon corporate merger.
- **Silence Risk**: Statutory bar on tenant assignments, eliminating organizational restructuring flexibility.

---

### [SP-6] Lock-in Period: Maximum Initial Lock-in and Exit Rights
- **Standard Position**: Initial lock-in period shall not exceed six (6) months. Following lock-in expiration, tenant may terminate at will with standard 60-day notice without penalty, forfeiture, or accelerated rent.
- **Acceptable Range**: Lock-in <= 6 months; post-lock-in exit permitted with 60-day notice; no accelerated rent penalties.
- **Departure Indicators**: Multi-year lock-in (12-36 months), full term accelerated rent liquidated damages, deposit forfeiture on early exit.
- **Silence Risk**: Inability to exit prior to full multi-year lease expiration without paying entire term rent.

---

### [SP-7] Termination and Default: Notice and Cure Periods
- **Standard Position**: In the event of default, tenant is entitled to written notice and a minimum cure period: thirty (30) days for monetary defaults, and forty-five (45) days for non-monetary defaults. Immediate forfeiture or lockout is prohibited.
- **Acceptable Range**: Monetary cure >= 30 days; non-monetary cure >= 45 days; written notice required.
- **Departure Indicators**: Cure windows under 30 days (e.g. 3-10 days), immediate landlord lockout remedies, waiver of written notice.
- **Silence Risk**: Summary eviction or forfeiture for minor bookkeeping delays or administrative disputes.

---

### [SP-8] Alterations and Improvements: Minor Cosmetic and Fitting Rights
- **Standard Position**: Tenant is entitled to make non-structural interior alterations, minor architectural updates, cabling, and branding up to $10,000 without prior landlord consent. No mandatory bare-shell restoration at lease end unless stipulated at approval.
- **Acceptable Range**: Non-structural alterations <= $10,000 permitted without consent; no arbitrary review fees; no bare-shell restoration requirement.
- **Departure Indicators**: Prior approval required for minor cosmetic changes, review fees charged by landlord, mandatory bare-shell restoration.
- **Silence Risk**: Landlord claims unauthorized alteration, demands immediate removal, or withholds deposit for normal tenant fit-outs.
